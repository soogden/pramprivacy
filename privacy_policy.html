Privacy Policy for Park Pram Grand Prix

Effective Date: November 22, 2026
Developer: ScottCorp
Application: Park Pram Grand Prix (com.scottcorp.pram)

1. Overview

Park Pram Grand Prix is an offline, single-player 3D mobile game developed by ScottCorp. We respect your privacy and designed the application so that it does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or device identifiers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The application operates entirely offline on your device and does not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (such as name, email address, phone number, or account credentials)
  • Device identifiers, Advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Location data (precise or coarse)
  • Analytics, crash telemetry, or behavioral tracking data
  • Payment or financial information (the app contains no in-app purchases or advertisements)

3. Device Permissions

The application requests only the following non-sensitive hardware capability:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide optional in-game haptic feedback during races (such as countdown ticks, kerb bumps, and baby mood alerts). Haptic feedback can be toggled off at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because Park Pram Grand Prix collects zero personal or device information from any user, it complies with applicable children’s privacy regulations including COPPA and GDPR-K.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of the application, any revisions will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact ScottCorp via the developer contact email listed on the official Google Play Store page for Park Pram Grand Prix.
